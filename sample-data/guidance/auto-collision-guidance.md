# Motor collision claims - investigation notes (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE - fictional procedure for demonstration only.

## Expected documents

A motor collision claim normally includes a completed claim form, an itemised repair estimate from a
workshop, and an incident report or exchange-of-details record.

## Consistency checks

- The repair estimate total should match the claimed amount. Labour, parts, and paint lines should add up
  to the stated total.
- The vehicle registration on the estimate should match the registration on the claim form.
- Damage described on the estimate should be consistent with the described point of impact.

## Common benign explanations

Workshops sometimes revise estimates after stripping a panel, which can change the total. A revised
estimate with a new reference number usually explains a difference in amounts.

## Suggested analyst actions

- Ask the workshop to confirm the latest estimate reference and total.
- Compare photographs of the damage with the estimate line items when available.
