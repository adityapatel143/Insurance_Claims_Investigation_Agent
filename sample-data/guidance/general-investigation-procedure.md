# General claim investigation procedure (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE - fictional procedure for demonstration only; not an actual insurer policy.

## Role of the investigation assistant

The assistant organises evidence for a licensed claims analyst. It never decides coverage, eligibility,
settlement, or outcome. Every finding is a prompt for analyst review.

## Baseline checks for every claim

1. Confirm that the policy reference on every document matches the submitted policy reference.
2. Confirm that the incident date is the same across the claim form, supporting reports, and invoices.
3. Confirm that the claimed amount equals the total on the supporting estimate or invoice.
4. Record any document that is missing, unreadable, or in an unsupported format as missing information.
5. Note the gap between the incident date and the reported date. A gap above 30 days is a potential
   anomaly signal that requires review; many delays have ordinary explanations such as travel or illness
   of a family member.

## Recommended analyst actions

- Request missing documents directly from the policyholder through the usual channel.
- Where amounts differ, request the itemised source document and check for arithmetic or data-entry errors.
- Where dates differ, ask for a short written timeline from the policyholder.
- Escalate to a senior analyst only when several independent signals point the same way.
