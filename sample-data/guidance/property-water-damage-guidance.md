# Property escape-of-water claims - investigation notes (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE - fictional procedure for demonstration only.

## Expected documents

A property water-damage claim normally includes a completed claim form and a contractor repair estimate.
Photographs and a plumber's report on the cause are helpful but optional.

## Consistency checks

- The cause described on the claim form should match the cause described by the contractor or plumber.
- The repair estimate should itemise flooring, cabinetry, and drying costs separately.
- The reported date should normally be close to the date the damage was discovered.

## Suggested analyst actions

- When the repair estimate is marked as "to follow", request it before the case progresses.
- Ask whether the appliance hose was retained for inspection.
- Confirm whether a neighbouring property was also affected.
