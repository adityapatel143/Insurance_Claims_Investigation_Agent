# Travel delayed-baggage claims - investigation notes (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC GUIDANCE - fictional procedure for demonstration only.

## Expected documents

A delayed-baggage claim normally includes a completed claim form, the airline property irregularity
reference, and itemised invoices or receipts for essential replacement purchases.

## Consistency checks

- Purchase dates on invoices should fall on or after the date the baggage was reported delayed.
- The invoice total should match the claimed amount, or the difference should be explained.
- Items purchased should be essentials appropriate to the length of the delay.

## Suggested analyst actions

- Request the airline's written confirmation of the delay duration.
- Where invoice dates differ from the incident date, ask the traveller for the purchase timeline.
- Where totals differ, ask whether some items were later returned or claimed elsewhere.
