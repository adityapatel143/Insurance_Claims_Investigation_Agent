# Historical example - motor estimate revised after inspection (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC HISTORICAL EXAMPLE - fictional case for retrieval demonstrations only.

A fictional low-speed car park collision claim showed a claimed amount 18 percent higher than the first
repair estimate. The analyst requested the latest estimate; the workshop confirmed a revised estimate
after removing the door trim revealed a bent hinge. The amounts then matched. Lesson: request the latest
estimate reference before treating an amount difference as significant.
