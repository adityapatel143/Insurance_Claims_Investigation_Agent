# Historical example - water damage reported without contractor estimate (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC HISTORICAL EXAMPLE - fictional case for retrieval demonstrations only.

A fictional apartment water-damage claim was submitted with only a claim form. The analyst requested a
contractor estimate and photographs. The estimate arrived ten days later and itemised flooring and drying
costs. Lesson: missing estimates are common early in property claims and should be requested promptly.
