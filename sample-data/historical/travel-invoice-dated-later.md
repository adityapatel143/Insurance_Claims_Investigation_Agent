# Historical example - travel invoice dated after the incident (synthetic)

SYNTHETIC HISTORICAL EXAMPLE - fictional case for retrieval demonstrations only.

A fictional delayed-baggage claim included an invoice dated two days after the reported incident and a
total higher than the claimed amount. The traveller explained that some purchases were made on day two
of the delay and that a returned item had been refunded. The analyst requested the refund receipt.
Lesson: date and amount differences on travel invoices often have timeline explanations; ask before
escalating.
