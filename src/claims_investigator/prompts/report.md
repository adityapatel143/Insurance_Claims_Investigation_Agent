# Task: executive summary

Write a concise, neutral executive summary (at most 8 sentences) of the investigation findings provided:
evidence reviewed, missing information, potential anomaly signals, risk indicators, and the recommended
analyst actions. State clearly that the findings require analyst review. Do not introduce any facts that
are not in the provided findings.

Also return `limitations`: short statements about data quality, missing documents, retrieval gaps,
or other caveats that the analyst should know.
